# Understanding Your Bill and Fees

## How billing works

- You're billed **monthly in advance** by **Direct Debit**, on the same day each month.
- Your **first bill** includes a pro-rata amount (covering the part-month until your regular billing date) plus your installation fee, if one applies.
- Direct Debit is currently the only supported payment method.

## Fee table

| Fee | Amount | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Migration charge | £17 | 30-day rolling contracts only, Openreach/CityFibre network transfer |
| Cessation charge | £60 | Openreach/Trooli/MS3 only, rolling from outset, cancelled within first 12 months |
| Reactivation after non-payment | £25 | Account suspended for non-payment, then reactivated |
| Failed/returned Direct Debit | £12 | Each time a DD payment fails or is returned |
| Speed tier change | Free, or £5 | Free normally; £5 if you've already changed tier within the last 90 days |
| CityFibre engineer/ONT replacement | £75 | CityFibre customers needing an engineer visit or ONT swap outside of a fault |
| Openreach Aborted Visit Charge | £105 | Openreach engineer visit aborted due to customer no-show or access issue |
| BGP setup | £50 one-off | Setting up a BGP session |
| BGP modification | £10 | Changes made more than 30 days after initial BGP setup |

## Early Termination Charges

None of the fees above are Early Termination Charges (ETCs). ETCs only apply if you cancel a fixed-term (12 or 24-month) contract early, see [Contract Types and Cancelling Explained](./contract-types-and-cancelling-explained) for the formula.
