# Network Management and Fair Use, Explained

The short version: **we don't throttle you, and there's no hidden fair-use cap.**

This is a plain-language summary of our full Network Management Policy, which follows the Open Internet Regulation (EU) 2015/2120. The full legal text lives at [/network-management](/network-management), this page is the readable version.

## What we don't do

- No throttling or shaping by protocol, port, or application (no deprioritising torrents, streaming, or gaming traffic)
- No fair-use data caps
- No deep packet inspection to shape or monetise your traffic

If your plan says unlimited, it's unlimited, full speed, all the time.

## What we do reserve the right to do (rarely)

Traffic management only ever happens for one of three reasons:

1. **Legal obligations**, e.g. blocking known child sexual abuse material using IWF blocklists, as UK law requires of all ISPs.
2. **Network integrity and security**, e.g. null-routing a device that's the target (or source) of a DDoS attack, or blocking spoofed traffic, to protect the network for everyone.
3. **Congestion management**, extremely rare, non-discriminatory queueing only, never targeted at specific customers, protocols, or apps.

## Abuse and complaints

If you believe your connection has been unfairly managed, or want to report abuse originating from the Olilo network:

- **Abuse reports:** abuse@olilo.co.uk
- **Everything else:** support@olilo.co.uk, then [/complaints](/complaints) if unresolved, then Ofcom if it's still not sorted
