# Getting Help Fast: Support Channels Explained

## Everyday support

| Channel | Hours | Typical response |
|---|---|---|
| **Discord**, discord.gg/olilo | 09:00-17:00 | Usually under 15 minutes |
| **Email**, support@olilo.co.uk | 09:00-17:00 | Within 4 hours |

There's currently no phone line or live chat: Discord is the fastest way to reach a human.

## Before you message support

Two things that answer most questions instantly:

- **status.olilo.co.uk**, live network status, check here first if something feels down
- **speedtest.olilo.co.uk**, Olilo's own speed test node, best way to test against your actual plan speed

## The right channel for the right thing

- **General questions, faults, setup help:** Discord or support@olilo.co.uk
- **Formal complaints:** complaints@olilo.co.uk, see [How to Make a Complaint](./how-to-make-a-complaint.md)
- **Abuse reports** (spam, attacks originating from our network): abuse@olilo.co.uk
- **Emergency services, if you're deaf/hard of hearing/speech-impaired:** 999 BSL (999bsl.co.uk), 24/7
