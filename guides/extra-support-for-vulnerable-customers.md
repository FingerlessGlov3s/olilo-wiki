# Extra Support for Vulnerable Customers

If you, or someone in your household, needs extra support, you don't need medical proof or paperwork to ask for it. You can self-report, ask a nominated representative to reach out for you, or our team can flag it if they notice while helping you.

## What's available, free

- Communications in alternative formats
- A nominated third-party contact who can speak to us on your behalf
- Longer support windows and extra time to pay or respond
- Written confirmation of anything agreed verbally
- Priority routing when you contact support
- Protection from disconnection while we work through options with you
- Signposting to specialist help: Citizens Advice, Samaritans, Refuge, RNIB, Action on Hearing Loss

## Financial hardship

If you're struggling to pay:

- We can set up a repayment plan
- Payments can be paused or deferred
- Late and failed-payment fees can be waived
- We'll signpost you to StepChange, Citizens Advice, or National Debtline
- We won't disconnect you without exploring these options first and giving reasonable notice

## Life-critical equipment

If you rely on telecare, dialysis, or similar equipment that needs a working connection, tell us, you'll get **Priority Fault Fix**. We'll also recommend keeping a backup mobile data connection as a safety net.

## Nominated representatives

We accept a nominated representative or Power of Attorney to manage your account on your behalf, just let us know.

## Domestic abuse support

We can add a safeword to your account, route contact through an intermediary, or help transfer your account away from a joint account holder. The **National Domestic Abuse Helpline** is free, 24/7: **0808 2000 247**.

## Deaf, hard of hearing, or speech-impaired?

Use **999 BSL** (999bsl.co.uk) for emergency services, available 24/7.
