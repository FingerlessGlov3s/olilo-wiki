# Contract Types and Cancelling Explained

## The three contract types

| Type | Install fee | Notice to cancel |
|---|---|---|
| **Alt+F4 Anytime™** (30-day rolling) | £80 | 30 days |
| 12-month | Free | Fixed term, see ETC below |
| 24-month | Free | Fixed term, see ETC below |

## Cancelling a rolling plan

Give **30 days' notice**, no Early Termination Charge. A £60 cessation fee can still apply on Openreach/Trooli/MS3 if you cancel within your first 12 months on a rolling plan, see [Understanding Your Bill and Fees](./understanding-your-bill-and-fees.md).

## Cancelling a fixed-term plan early

If you leave a 12 or 24-month contract before it ends, the Early Termination Charge (ETC) is:

```
Remaining months × your monthly price
```

For example, 4 months left on a £45/month plan = £180 ETC. This only applies to fixed-term contracts, rolling plans never have an ETC.

## Price-rise protection

- **During your minimum term**, your price cannot rise mid-contract.
- **After your minimum term ends**, Olilo can change your price with at least **30 days' notice**. If you don't accept the new price, you can cancel penalty-free.
- **Rolling customers** always get at least 30 days' notice of any price change.
