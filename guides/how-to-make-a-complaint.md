# How to Make a Complaint

If something's gone wrong and support hasn't fixed it, here's how to raise a formal complaint and what to expect.

## How to raise one

- **Email:** complaints@olilo.co.uk (or support@olilo.co.uk if you're not sure it needs to be formal yet)
- **Discord:** discord.gg/olilo
- **Forum:** terminal.olilo.co.uk
- **Post:** Olilo UK & Ireland Ltd, Lancaster House, Amy Johnson Way, Blackpool, FY4 2RP

There's currently no telephone complaints line.

## What happens next

1. **Acknowledged within 2 working days**, with a reference number.
2. **Resolution timescale:**
   - Simple complaints: **5 working days**
   - Complex complaints: up to **8 weeks**
   - Urgent or service-affecting issues are prioritised same-day

## If you're not happy with the outcome

1. **Ask for a senior manager review** internally first.
2. If it's been **8 weeks** since you first complained, or Olilo has sent you a **deadlock letter** confirming there's nothing more we can do, you can take it to **CISAS**, the independent alternative dispute resolution scheme. It's free to you, and any decision is binding on Olilo (not on you, you can still pursue other routes if you disagree).
   - **CISAS phone:** 020 7536 6000
   - **CISAS email:** applications@cedr.com
3. You can also refer unresolved issues to **Ofcom**.
   - **Phone:** 0300 123 3333
   - **Textphone:** 0300 123 3393

Complaint records are kept for 18 months.
