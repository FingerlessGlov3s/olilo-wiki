# Moving House with Olilo

## Tell us at least 2 weeks before you move

Give Olilo at least **two weeks' notice** of your move date. This gives us time to arrange service at your new address and avoid a gap in connectivity.

## What happens at your new address

Moving house means a **new contract** at the new address, it isn't simply a transfer. That's because availability, network type, and pricing all depend on the address itself. Depending on what's available where you're moving to:

- Your minimum term may reset
- An installation/activation fee may apply if one wasn't charged before (or vice versa)
- Your network (CityFibre, Openreach, Freedom Fibre, etc.) may be different, check [Which Network Am I On?](./which-network-am-i-on.md) once you know

See T&Cs Section 22 for the full terms.

## Before you move

1. Confirm your new address is serviceable, check [/coverage](/coverage) or ask support.
2. Let support know your move date, at least 2 weeks ahead.
3. Ask what, if anything, changes about your plan, price, or contract at the new address.
